Member Replacement

Create the replacement member first in User Management, then select them here. The replacement must pay the exact exit settlement value (departing savings + profit + advance). That payment funds the exiting member even if society bank cash alone is insufficient. Histories stay intact.

Select a departing member to load the calculated exit settlement / entry valuation.

Member Exit & Share Redistribution

CEO initiates exit only. The departing member must approve first, then all remaining members approve the proportional share redistribution. After unanimous approval, the Cashier pays the settlement — CEO cannot disburse.
